# Projects

## Project 1: Instagram Saving Behavior and Personality Traits
- Project Description: Survey study (n=303) examining how Big Five personality traits relate to saving and bookmarking behavior on Instagram.
- Technologies Used: IPIP-NEO-50, Qualtrics, SPSS

## Project 2: Deferred Trust in AI Use By Students
- Project Description: A 2×2 vignette experiment (factual vs. moral dilemma × low vs. high criticality) testing when students defer to an AI agent over an institutional human advisor. Vignettes are adapted from real situations rather than invented scenarios.
- Technologies Used: Qualtrics, SPSS

## Project 3: Personality-Agent Co-Evolution
- Project Description: A conceptual framework for the bidirectional relationship between human personality and AI agents, with a focus on adolescents in their identity-formative years. Serves as the connective thread across my research program.

## Project 4: DoTheDream Youth Development Initiative — UX Redesign
- Project Description: Information architecture and content strategy redesign for a youth development nonprofit, from research through mockups.
- Technologies Used: Figma, user research, content strategy
